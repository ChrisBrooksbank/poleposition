# Audio System

## Overview

Procedural audio synthesis system using Web Audio API that recreates the sound design of the original Pole Position arcade, including engine sounds, sound effects, voice samples, and chiptune music.

## User Stories

- As a player, I want to hear the engine pitch rise and fall with my speed so driving feels visceral
- As a player, I want audio feedback for collisions, tire screeches, and track hazards
- As a player, I want to hear the iconic jingles and voice announcements from the original game

## Requirements

### Engine Sound (Procedural Synthesis)
- [ ] Continuous engine tone generated by oscillator (sawtooth/square wave)
- [ ] Frequency mapped to car speed: higher pitch = faster
- [ ] Pitch drops when releasing throttle (deceleration)
- [ ] Engine idle sound when stationary
- [ ] Smooth frequency transitions (no abrupt pitch jumps)

### Tire Screech
- [ ] Filtered noise (LFSR-based) triggered during sharp turns
- [ ] Intensity varies with turn sharpness
- [ ] Only plays when car is on the road (not on grass)

### Collision / Explosion Sound
- [ ] LFSR noise burst with decay envelope (~3 seconds)
- [ ] Triggered on collision with cars or billboards
- [ ] Matching the Namco 54XX character

### Discrete Sound Effects
- [ ] Credit/coin insert sound (short ascending tone, ~2s)
- [ ] Qualifying start fanfare (~6s chiptune jingle)
- [ ] Start sign countdown beeps (~2-5s beep sequence)
- [ ] Qualifying complete - non-pole (~4s jingle)
- [ ] Qualifying complete - pole position (~4s triumphant jingle)
- [ ] Race complete jingle (~4s)
- [ ] Time extend sound (~3s ascending arpeggio)
- [ ] Puddle hit sound (~1s white noise splash)
- [ ] Time bonus tick (~1s short beep)
- [ ] Overtake bonus tick (~1s short beep, different pitch)
- [ ] Roadside driving rumble (~3s noise, loops while on grass)

### Voice Samples
- [ ] "Qualifying Start" voice announcement (~3s)
- [ ] "Grand Prix Start" voice announcement (~4s)
- [ ] Apply 4-bit downsampling and low-pass filter to match retro Namco 52XX quality

### Music (Chiptune / Wavetable Style)
- [ ] Name entry music - 1st place (~27s loop)
- [ ] Name entry music - 2nd-6th place (~27s loop, different melody)
- [ ] Name entry music - 7th-100th place (~38s loop, different melody)
- [ ] Game over music (~10s melody)
- [ ] All music uses wavetable synthesis (8 voices max for authenticity)
- [ ] NO music during active gameplay (only engine + SFX)

### Audio Mixing
- [ ] Multiple simultaneous sounds without clipping
- [ ] Engine sound plays continuously during gameplay, other sounds layer on top
- [ ] Volume balancing between engine, SFX, and music
- [ ] Master volume control

### Stereo / Spatial Audio (Stretch Goal)
- [ ] AI cars' engines audible with stereo separation as player passes them
- [ ] Pan based on relative position (left/right of player)

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Engine sound pitch smoothly tracks car speed
- [ ] All 19 sound effects from research.md are implemented
- [ ] Voice announcements play at correct game state transitions
- [ ] Music plays during name entry and game over (not during gameplay)
- [ ] No audio glitches, pops, or clipping during normal gameplay
- [ ] Audio can be muted/unmuted (attract sound setting)

## Out of Scope

- Quadraphonic speaker configuration (original sit-down cabinet feature)
- Analog circuit emulation (we use digital synthesis approximation)
- MIDI output
- Custom sound editor
